Kyoto, Japan


Kyoto is widely considered the cultural heart of Japan. Unlike the neon-lit skyscrapers of Tokyo, Kyoto offers a serene glimpse into the past with its thousands of Buddhist temples, Shinto shrines, and traditional wooden teahouses. It is a city where you can still spot geiko and maiko walking through the historic Gion district, especially during the breathtaking cherry blossom season.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. The Fushimi Inari Shrine and its famous gates.
  2. Kinkaku-ji, the breathtaking Golden Pavilion.
  3. The Arashiyama Bamboo Grove walking path.
  4. Kiyomizu-dera temple with city views.
  5. The historic Gion District at night.

Must-Try Foods

  • Kaiseki Ryori: Traditional multi-course dinner.
  • Matcha: Premium green tea powder.
  • Yudofu: Freshly made boiled tofu.
  • Kyoto Ramen: Rich and savory noodle soup.
  • Mochi: Soft and sweet rice cakes.
