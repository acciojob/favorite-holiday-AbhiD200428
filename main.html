Kyoto, Japan


Kyoto is widely considered the cultural heart of Japan. Unlike the neon-lit skyscrapers of Tokyo, Kyoto offers a serene glimpse into the past with its thousands of Buddhist temples, Shinto shrines, and traditional wooden teahouses. It is a city where you can still spot geiko and maiko walking through the historic Gion district, especially during the breathtaking cherry blossom season.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. The Fushimi Inari Shrine with its thousands of vibrant orange torii gates.
  2. Kinkaku-ji, also known as the Golden Pavilion, which is covered in gold leaf.
  3. The towering Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, a truly otherworldly walking experience.
  4. Kiyomizu-dera, a historic temple offering spectacular views of the city.
  5. The Gion District, famous for its traditional machiya architecture and tea culture.

Must-Try Foods

  • Kaiseki Ryori: A traditional multi-course Japanese dinner.
  • Matcha Green Tea: Best enjoyed with handmade wagashi (sweets).
  • Yudofu: A simple yet delicious boiled tofu dish specific to Kyoto.
  • Ramen: Specifically the thick and creamy broths found near Kyoto Station.
  • Mochi: Especially the yatsuhashi variety flavored with cinnamon.
